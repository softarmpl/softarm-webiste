A few days ago I got sad news from a client. They decided to shut the project down.

We had worked on the app for a good few years. The last two were mostly a fight for customers.

Everything looked fine. Thousands of people used the app. They logged in, used it, came back. But hardly anyone paid.

Even during development I could see we were improving things that already worked. We added features. We improved existing ones. We optimised the user experience. Free users had access to the same advanced filters and analytics as paying ones.

Infrastructure costs grew with the number of users. Revenue didn't.

Nobody said: let's first check whether people want to pay for this.

The payments module was one of the last features we built.

I think that says it all.
