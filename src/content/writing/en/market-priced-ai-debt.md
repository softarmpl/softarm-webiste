Day two at GITEX AI in Berlin.

Next to the booths building things with AI, there are booths selling the cleanup. Messages like "AI builds it, we verify it." and a bunch of AI security and compliance companies.

A whole category of companies living off the fact that someone shipped fast and now can't tell whether it passes an audit.

The market priced in the technical debt of AI-generated code before most companies noticed it in their own.
